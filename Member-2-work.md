# Member 2 Contribution

## Task 1 — Railway Level-Crossing Constraints

### C6: Barrier must open only after the train has completely cleared the crossing

Reason:
Opening the barrier before the train has completely cleared the crossing could create a dangerous situation.

### C7: The system must not rely on a failed train-detection sensor

Reason:
A failed sensor may provide incorrect information and could cause unsafe barrier operation.

### C8: A barrier failure must be detected by the safety-monitoring unit

Reason:
The system must detect barrier failures so that appropriate safety action can be taken.

### C9: Communication loss must not cause the barrier to open unsafely

Reason:
Loss of communication must not put road users or trains at risk.

### C10: Emergency conditions must place the crossing into a safe state

Reason:
During an emergency, the system must prioritize the safety of trains, drivers, and pedestrians.
