# Task 1 — Identify Constraints

## C1: Barrier must not open while a train is present
Reason: Opening the barrier while a train is present could allow road traffic to enter the crossing and cause an accident.

## C2: Barrier must close when an approaching train is detected
Reason: The barrier must prevent road traffic from entering the crossing before the train arrives.

## C3: Warning lights must be activated when a train approaches
Reason: Warning lights alert drivers and pedestrians that a train is approaching.

## C4: Audible alarm must be activated when a train approaches
Reason: The alarm provides an additional warning to road users, including people who may not notice the warning lights.

## C5: Barrier must remain closed while the train is passing
Reason: Keeping the barrier closed prevents road traffic from entering the crossing while the train is passing.

## C6: Barrier must open only after the train has completely cleared the crossing
Reason: Opening the barrier before the train has completely cleared the crossing could create a dangerous situation.

## C7: The system must not rely on a failed train-detection sensor
Reason: A failed sensor may provide incorrect information and could cause unsafe barrier operation.

## C8: A barrier failure must be detected by the safety-monitoring unit
Reason: The system must detect barrier failures so that appropriate safety action can be taken.

## C9: Communication loss must not cause the barrier to open unsafely
Reason: Loss of communication must not put road users or trains at risk.

## C10: Emergency conditions must place the crossing into a safe state
Reason: During an emergency, the system must prioritize the safety of trains, drivers, and pedestrians.
